# Registrar v1.2.1

A small release for the landing page at <https://ceu.tools>.

## What's new

- **"How each body counts" fits your screen.** The per-body rules used to
  sit in a wide table you had to scroll sideways. On a phone or a narrow
  window each body is now a card: its name, unit and credentials, then
  the cycle, the cycle total, the annual minimum, categories and caps, how
  you submit, and a link to the body's own policy page. On a wide screen
  the same entries line up as a comparison grid that uses the width
  available, up to 1200 px. Nothing scrolls sideways at any width.
- **Show only your bodies.** Tick the bodies you hold (ISC2, ISACA,
  CompTIA, ...) and the section shows just those, with a count such as
  "3 of 10 bodies shown". **All** shows every body again. It works from the
  keyboard (Tab, then Space to tick).
- **One source for the rules.** The section is now generated from
  Registrar's rules file, `bodies.yaml`, the same data the browser app
  uses, so the page and the app cannot disagree. The figures and the
  UNVERIFIED badges are unchanged.

## Worth knowing

- The landing page still runs no scripts and sets no cookies. That is why
  the filter does not remember your choice: a reload shows every body
  again, and the page does not read what you picked in the app.
- Rules change. Confirm anything you rely on with your body's own portal.
