# Registrar v1.2.0

Registrar tracks the continuing education (CPEs, CEUs, PDUs) your
certifications require, with each body's caps, minimums and deadlines
applied. This release is about the first five minutes: the site explains
the problem, the app asks what you hold, and there is sample data to look
at before you type anything. The landing page now carries a CPE/CEU guide.
It also adds reports you can open in a spreadsheet or a calendar.

Try it at <https://ceu.tools/app>. Nothing to install, no account, and your
data stays in your browser.

## What's new

### Start in a minute

- **"Which credentials do you hold?"** A new browser asks this first (you
  can skip it). Your progress, the activity form, Settings, Rules, the
  calendar and the reports follow from the answer. You can give each
  credential's cycle start date now or later; until you do, the
  Credentials screen asks for it.
- **Sample data.** On an empty ledger, **Load sample data** adds three
  credentials from three bodies (CISSP, CySA+ and CFE) and nine invented
  activities with generated certificates. You can see a Group B cap
  forfeiting hours, a Group A shortfall, a conference cap, a CFE with all
  20 hours but a fraud minimum still short, an activity waiting for
  confirmation and one with no evidence. Everything is titled "SAMPLE —".
  **Remove sample data** deletes exactly those rows and nothing of yours.
- **A certificate to try capture with.** The empty ledger links a sample
  certificate: drop it on Log activity and watch the date, hours, provider
  and title fill in, read on your device.

### Reports, made in your browser

- **Activities (.csv)**: every activity with its evidence file names. Opens
  in any spreadsheet as UTF-8, and cells that look like formulas are
  neutralised.
- **Workbook (.xlsx)**: Activities, Evidence and Totals sheets. The Totals
  sheet holds the same figures as the Credentials screen: counted, naive
  sum, forfeited, verdict. It is built without any spreadsheet library.
- **Cycle ends calendar (.ics)**: one all-day event per credential you hold,
  on the last day of its cycle, with alerts 90 and 30 days before. Its
  events have the same IDs as the self-hosted app's calendar, so importing
  both updates one event instead of adding two.

The JSON **Export** at the top stays the backup format. It is unchanged:
a v1.1.0 export imports and re-exports to the same bytes.

### Know what you are running

- The footer shows the version and the build's content hash, linked to
  these notes.
- **ceu.tools** explains the problem before the tool. It covers who
  Registrar is for, the seven credentials covered, what an UNVERIFIED badge
  means, and how your data stays local, with screenshots and a short
  capture demo. It still runs no scripts.
- **A CPE/CEU guide** on ceu.tools: how cycles, Group A and Group B and
  caps work, keeping audit-ready records, what usually does not count, your
  first 90 days with a new certification, where to earn credits, a table
  of how each body counts, and a glossary. It is adapted from
  [CPE Resources](https://insiderthreatanalyst.com/cpe) (CC BY 4.0), and
  every figure in it comes from the same rules file the app uses.
- **ceu.tools/privacy** gives the threat model, and exactly what the
  optional bring-your-own-key feature sends and to whom: the text of one
  certificate, to the provider you chose, only when you press the button.
  Never the file, and the key is never stored.
- `/.well-known/security.txt` points to GitHub private vulnerability
  reporting. Link previews and app icons are added.

### Accessibility

- An axe-core audit of every screen of the site and the app, in light and
  dark mode, reports no violations. One was found and fixed: a duplicate
  landmark label on the landing page.
- The core flow works with the keyboard alone, with a visible focus ring
  at every step: first run, sample data, reading a certificate, saving an
  activity, downloading a report and exporting.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Existing browser data is kept, and the first-run question
is not shown to a browser that already holds activities or credentials.
The self-hosted Python app is unchanged in this release.

## Known limitations

- Rules marked UNVERIFIED are still unverified. Check them against your
  body's portal.
- The evidence parser was tuned on synthetic certificates. On held-out
  text it gets 25 of 30 fields right. Always check what it fills in.
- OCR was tested on rendered certificates, not on real phone photos.
- One browser profile holds one ledger. Moving between devices is by
  Export and Import.
- The bring-your-own-key feature has not yet been tested against the live
  provider APIs.

## Verification

- Unit tests (browser app): 52, including the reports read back and the
  v1.1.0 byte-compatibility fixture.
- End-to-end, in headless Chromium under the site's real
  Content-Security-Policy: 57 checks on the app and 38 on the site,
  and an axe accessibility scan with no violations. Three
  deliberately broken builds and two broken sites must fail them, and do.
- Python suite: unchanged in scope, all green.
