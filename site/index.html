ceu.tools

Tools for keeping your professional credentials current.

Every certifying body counts continuing education differently: caps, minimums, windows, its own name for the unit. These tools do that arithmetic so you do not find out at renewal time.

Early release. Registrar works today, but it is young and it is self-host only: there is no hosted version yet. Some bodies' rules could not be confirmed against their own published pages; the app marks those as unverified and does not hide it. Check anything important against your body's portal.

The suite

Tool #1

Registrar

Track your CPEs, CEUs and PDUs in one place.

  • Totals that respect each body's caps and category minimums, and show you what a plain sum of hours would have got wrong.
  • Evidence files hashed when you attach them and re-checked before you need them.
  • A deadline calendar (.ics) for cycle ends, grace periods, registration windows and fees.
  • Submission packets: each activity laid out in your body's form fields, plus a zip of your evidence. You sign in and submit yourself; Registrar never asks for your portal login.
  • Optional Telegram capture with your own bot.
Self-host Registrar

Python 3 standard library only. Runs on your own machine or private network, bound to localhost by default. Free software under the GNU AGPL v3.
